About Dr. Shridhar Joshi

Dr. Shridhar Joshi is a child neurologist dedicated to the care of infants, children and teenagers with conditions of the brain, nerves and muscles. He believes parents are partners in care, so every visit includes a clear explanation of what is happening and what to expect next.

From the first consultation to long-term follow-up, his approach combines careful examination, evidence-based treatment and a calm, reassuring manner that helps children feel at ease.

Core child neurology services

🧠

Epilepsy and seizures

Careful evaluation of seizures in infants, children and teenagers, with clear treatment plans and follow-up that parents can understand.

🌱

Developmental delay

Assessment of delayed speech, walking or learning milestones, with guidance on therapies and the support your child needs at home and school.

💬

Autism and ADHD

Evaluation of attention, behaviour and social communication concerns, with coordinated care alongside therapists and educators.

🤕

Headache in children

Finding the cause of recurring headaches in children, and a treatment approach that fits school and family life.

🦵

Cerebral palsy and movement

Ongoing care for children with movement, muscle tone and posture difficulties, working together with physiotherapy and rehabilitation teams.

⚡

EEG and diagnostics

Tests such as EEG and brain imaging are advised only when needed, and every result is explained in plain language.

Why choose Dr. Shridhar Joshi

Child-friendly, compassionate care

Every child is different. Dr. Joshi takes time to listen to parents, explains each step in simple words, and keeps check-ups comfortable for children.

Evidence-based approach

Diagnosis and treatment follow current, trusted medical guidelines, with tests and medicines recommended only when they are truly needed.

Privacy and confidentiality

Your child’s health information and consultations are handled with discretion and respect, and shared only with your permission.
